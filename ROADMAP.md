# Roadmap

## Available

- Local capability profiles and eligibility checks.
- SymPy and fixed-host WeatherNext adapters.
- Optional approved evidence comparison and abstention.
- Offline synthetic policy and ranking evaluations.

## Next

- Improve reproducible setup and fixture documentation.
- Bind real, independently reviewable benchmark results to exact artifacts and deployments.
- Improve approval ledger integrity and expose clearer comparison reasons.

## Longer term

- Additional discovery connectors, execution adapters and domain validators, subject to security and evidence review.
- Real-data backtesting and uncertainty calibration.
